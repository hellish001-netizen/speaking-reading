Unit 1: Passive Voice

Recognizing passive structures in National Exam reading passages

1. Core Concept & Formula

In passive sentences, the action or receiver of the action becomes the main subject.

General Formula:
Subject + Be + Past Participle (Verb 3) [+ by Agent]
Tense Active Voice Passive Voice
Simple Present They clean the lab daily. The lab is cleaned daily.
Simple Past He wrote the report. The report was written.

2. High-Yield Exam Traps ⚠️

Trap #1: Irregular Verbs National Exams test irregular Past Participles (V3). Avoid choices with V2 or base forms (e.g., was wrote ❌ vs. was written ✅).
Trap #2: Subject-Verb Agreement Verify if the target subject is singular or plural:
Example: "The announcements (plural) were posted," NOT "was posted."
